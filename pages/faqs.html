Useful to know

A few things people ask.

How do I choose a programme?

Start with the language, your current experience, and what you hope to do with it. Contact the team to discuss suitable options.

Are courses online or in person?

Online and classroom formats are offered for selected programmes. Ask about the course and schedule you need.

How do I find my level?

The team can discuss your previous learning and may recommend a placement assessment for relevant programmes.

Can I prepare for an exam?

Preparation is available for selected language exams. Confirm current options with the course team and requirements with the exam provider.

How do I pay?

Ask for the current fee and written payment instructions for your programme. Verify all payment details directly with Langma.
