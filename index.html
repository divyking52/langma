Why Langma

Guidance with a point of view.

A platform connecting learning, education, careers, mobility, culture and business — with experienced trainers and advice shaped to the person, not a template.

01

Personalised courses

Every journey is unique. Our team provides customized guidance aligned with your aspirations. Students, professionals, diplomats, corporations, government staff and migration aspirants each receive programmes designed for their context.

02

Flexible timing

  • Online live classes
  • Campus classes in South Extension, New Delhi
  • Weekend batches
  • Fast-track programmes
  • Corporate groups, on-site or online
03

One-to-one sessions

Fully personalised sessions at your pace, schedule and focus. Langma describes these as suited to diplomats, executives and learners with specific communication needs. Small batches keep individual attention in group classes too.

04

Placement pathways

Global placement connects skilled candidates with verified employers. Work-abroad support covers job-search direction, documents, interviews and relocation guidance. Langma’s own words: no false promises, no shortcuts — and an honest word when a route may not suit your profile. Availability depends on destination, employer and profile.

About Langma

From language learning to global opportunities.

Language has the power to connect worlds.

Langma International began as a foreign language training initiative and describes itself today as a global opportunities platform for students, professionals, institutions, businesses and investors. Established in 2012, in South Extension, New Delhi, and online worldwide.

Vision

To become a globally trusted platform that empowers people and organizations to learn, connect, grow and thrive across cultures, industries and nations.

Mission

  • Expanding access to global learning opportunities.
  • Building international education pathways.
  • Supporting career mobility and professional growth.
  • Promoting cultural understanding and global citizenship.
  • Facilitating business collaboration across borders.
